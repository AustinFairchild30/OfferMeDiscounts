This page documents the exact opt-in step on OfferMeDiscounts.com for SMS compliance review. Opt-in methods

  • Website form (shown above) — visitor enters their phone number and taps "Text Me the Code" on index.html. Entering a phone number and tapping this button only triggers a one-time, transactional verification text confirming the number — it is not a marketing message and requires no separate consent. Recurring marketing texts (this deal plus future personalized deals) are a distinct, unchecked-by-default checkbox directly above the button; a visitor must actively check it to opt in, and can complete the entire unlock flow and receive their code without ever checking it.
  • Keyword/text-in — a visitor may also text our number directly to begin; the first automated reply repeats the same consent and opt-out language before any marketing message is sent.
