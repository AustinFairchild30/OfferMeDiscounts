About OfferMeDiscounts

OfferMeDiscounts.com is a deal-discovery site built around a simple idea: unlocking a coupon should take one text, not ten browser tabs. Instead of hunting through expired codes and pop-up ads, you pick a deal, verify your phone number once, and get a working code — on screen and in a text.

How it works

  1. Browse or search deals across categories like electronics, food, travel, and more.
  2. Verify your number with a one-time code sent by text — this confirms you're a real person and opts you into future deal alerts.
  3. Get your code instantly, shown on screen and sent by text, ready to use at checkout.

You can opt out of texts at any time by replying STOP — see our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy for the details.

Where the deals come from

Today's catalog is hand-curated. We're in the process of applying to affiliate networks so future deals come directly from real retailer partnerships — when that happens, this page will be updated to reflect it.
