About OfferMeDiscounts

OfferMeDiscounts.com is built around a simple idea: you shouldn't have to go hunting for deals. Tell us what you're into once, and from then on the deals come to you — sorted around what you actually like, and texted to you the moment something new matches.

Unlocking a code takes one text, not ten browser tabs. No account, no password, no inbox full of newsletters you didn't ask for.

How it works

  1. Tap what you like. Right on the front page — brands and styles, no sign-up, nothing to fill in. The deals reorder around your picks as you go.
  2. Unlock a code with a one-time text. This confirms you're a real person, and it's how we keep codes working instead of letting them get scraped and burnt out.
  3. Get matched from then on. We text you when a new deal lands that fits what you told us — no digging required.

You can opt out of texts at any time by replying STOP — see our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy for the details.

How we learn what you like

Most coupon sites show everyone the same wall of offers. We'd rather show you a shorter list that's actually relevant, and we build that three ways:

  • What you tap. The picker on the front page works before you've given us anything at all — like a few brands or styles and the deal list re-sorts immediately. Those picks stay in your browser until you decide to register.
  • What you tell us. When you unlock your first code we ask which categories and brands you care about. Specific beats broad: "Skincare" gets you better matches than "Beauty".
  • What you actually use. The deals you open and the codes you copy count for more than anything you declare, and telling us you're not interested steers future matches away.

You can also just ask. The search box understands plain language — "a gift for my mom", "something for a beach trip" — rather than only matching brand names you already know.

Where the deals come from

Every offer on this site comes from a direct affiliate partnership with the retailer, through the CJ Affiliate and Impact networks. Nothing is scraped from other coupon sites, and no code is invented or guessed at.

The catalog re-syncs from our partners automatically every day. New offers arrive, expired ones are deleted, and every outbound link is followed to the retailer's own site to confirm it still resolves — a link that fails two days running is pulled from the site. Dead codes are the usual complaint about coupon sites, and this is how we keep them off ours.

For brands and partners

We're not a search-arbitrage coupon site. The offer is the acquisition hook; the asset is an opted-in messaging audience.

  • Every contact is verified and explicitly opted in. A one-time password confirms the number really belongs to the person entering it, and ongoing marketing consent is a separate, unchecked checkbox — never bundled into the verification step.
  • Built for TCPA compliance from the start. Express written consent, honored STOP and HELP handling, and a toll-free number vetted and approved by the carriers. Our opt-in proof page documents the exact flow.
  • Offers are matched, not blasted. Subscribers tell us the brands and categories they care about, and we send what fits — which keeps list health up and puts your offer in front of people who asked for that kind of thing.
  • Traffic is sent through your tracked links, so every click and conversion is attributable in your own network reporting.
